# THE LAST VARIABLE — TpT Listing Copy & Go-To-Market

## Product brand
**THE LAST VARIABLE** — a middle-school math escape game.

## SEO listing title (front-loaded for TpT search)
> Middle School Math Escape Room Game | Differentiated 7th Grade Review | Team vs Hunter Print & Play Board Game

## Listing description (top — lead with the moat)

> **Designed by a special education paraprofessional.** Most math games let the strongest student win while everyone else watches. *The Last Variable* makes that impossible. It's an asymmetrical escape game: a team of **Circuit Breakers** races to reboot a collapsing mainframe and escape, while one player — **The Glitch** — hunts them down.
>
> The twist: the split-information mechanic means every player is *required*, not just included. Three built-in difficulty tiers let students at different levels share the same board and pull from the same deck — nobody is visibly in "the easy group." Using your character means doing the math, so kids practice fractions, algebra, geometry, and percentages because they *want* to fire their powers.
>
> Print, cut, and play. Low prep, high engagement, 7th-grade aligned (7.NS, 7.EE, 7.RP, 7.G).
