# THE LAST VARIABLE — Rules of Play

A middle-school math escape game for **4–5 players** (1 teacher-facilitated table or small group). One player is **The Glitch** (the hunter). The rest are **Circuit Breakers** (the team). The Breakers must reboot the mainframe and escape before the Glitch deletes them — before one of them becomes *the last variable*.

---

## The Story

The school's mainframe is collapsing. A rogue program — **The Glitch** — is deleting everything inside it, one variable at a time. A team of **Circuit Breakers** is trapped in the system. To escape, they have to bring three generators back online and reach the exit node. The Glitch is hunting them the whole time. Solve the math, trip the circuit, get out.

---

## Components (print-and-play)

- 1 game board (the Mainframe map)
- 11 character cards — 8 Circuit Breakers, 3 Glitches
- 4 problem decks (Fractions, Algebra, Geometry, Percentages), each split into **Tier 1 / Tier 2 / Tier 3**
- Answer keys (one per deck)
- Token punch-out sheet (player tokens, generator markers, freeze markers, cooldown markers)
- Role-rotation cards
- 1 round timer (sand timer, phone, or the included turn track)

---

## Setup (5 minutes)

1. Lay out the Mainframe board. Place the **3 generator markers** on the marked nodes and the **exit node** token.
2. Deal roles: 1 player draws **The Glitch** (use the rotation cards — see *Rotating Roles*). The rest are **Circuit Breakers**.
3. **Pick characters.** Each Breaker chooses 1 of the 8 Breaker cards (no duplicates). The Glitch chooses 1 of the 3 Glitch cards.
4. **Set difficulty tiers.** Each player has a tier (1 = building, 2 = on-level, 3 = challenge) — assign them to match each student, or let players choose. Different tiers share the table; they just draw from different parts of the deck. **The Glitch defaults to Tier 3**, but a student who needs it may drop one tier (see *Rotating Roles*).
5. Place all tokens on the **Start node**. Set the turn track to **10**. (5 players? The Glitch starts with **+1 move**.)

---

## How a Turn Works

Play goes around the table: each Breaker takes a turn, then the Glitch takes a turn. That's one round. The turn track counts down each round.

**On a Breaker's turn:**
1. **Move** up to 3 nodes along the lines.
2. **(Optional) Activate your power** — see *Powers*.
3. **Interact** with whatever you're standing on (a generator, a door, a teammate).

**On the Glitch's turn:**
1. **Move** up to 4 nodes (the Glitch is faster).
2. **(Optional) Activate your power.**
3. If the Glitch is on the same node as a Breaker, that Breaker is **frozen** (place a freeze marker). A frozen Breaker stays frozen for **1 full round** before any revive is allowed, then can't move or act until revived.

---

## Powers — the engine of the game

Every power costs a **solve**. To activate a power:

1. Draw a problem card from the matching skill deck, **at your tier**.
2. Solve it out loud or on paper.
3. Check the answer key. **Correct → the power fires. Wrong → the power fizzles and your turn ends.**

This is the whole point: using your character means doing math. A Tier-1 player and a Tier-3 player can both fire the same power — they just pull different cards. Nobody is locked out of being awesome because of their level.

Most powers have a **cooldown** (place a cooldown marker; it clears after the listed number of rounds). See each character card for power, skill, and cooldown.

**Tier reward — harder math, more powers.** Solve at **Tier 3** and that power's cooldown is reduced by **1 round** (minimum 0). Choosing the challenge tier means you get to use your ability more often. (This is why picking an easy tier to coast doesn't pay off.)

---

## Objectives & Winning

**Generators need two Breakers.** Each generator node is an objective. To bring one online, **two different Breakers** must each solve a problem from the matching deck at their own tier — one holds half the reboot code, the other holds the rest. They don't have to solve on the same turn: place a half-marker on the first correct solve, and flip the generator **online** when the second Breaker lands their solve. **No one solos the objectives** — the team has to split up and cooperate, and every player is needed.

**The Glitch speeds up (rubber-band).** Each time a generator comes online, the Glitch permanently gains **+1 move** per turn. Once **all 3** are online, freezes last **2 rounds** before any revive. The closer the Breakers get to escaping, the more dangerous the hunt.

**The Circuit Breakers win** when **all 3 generators are online AND at least one Breaker reaches any exit node** before the turn track runs out.

**The Glitch wins** if the turn track runs out before the Breakers escape, **OR** every Breaker is frozen at the same time.

**Reviving.** After its 1-round freeze lockout, a frozen Breaker is revived by **The Medic's Reboot** (Medic must be within 2 nodes), or by any teammate who moves onto the frozen Breaker's node and solves a Percentages card at their tier.

---

## Rotating Roles (anti-pile-on rule)

Use the **rotation deck**: shuffle it, and the role of Glitch passes to the next player each game. **No player is "the Glitch" two games in a row, and everyone plays the Glitch before anyone repeats.** The Glitch is the boss role, not a punishment and not a reward — it rotates by the cards, not by who's "best at math."

Teachers: if you want a specific student to get the Glitch's harder Tier-3 problems, seed the rotation deck quietly during setup. The table just sees the cards decide.

**If a struggling student rotates into the Glitch:** let them play at **Tier 2** instead of Tier 3. The Glitch is still timed and still the boss — nobody should be crushed in the hunter seat. A student who *wants* the Tier-3 challenge keeps the full timer; dropping to Tier 2 tightens the timer a little, so it stays a fair trade either way.

## Quick Reference

| | Breakers | The Glitch |
|---|---|---|
| Players | 3–4 | 1 |
| Move per turn | 3 nodes | 4 nodes, **+1 per generator online** |
| Problem tier | assigned/choice (1–3); **T3 solve = −1 cooldown** | Tier 3 default (timed), may drop to T2 |
| Generators | **2 Breakers each solve** to bring 1 online | — |
| Win by | all 3 generators + reach any exit | run out the clock OR freeze all |

**Turn track:** starts at **10**. **5 players?** The Glitch starts with **+1 move**. Freezes hold 1 round before revive (2 rounds once all generators are online).
