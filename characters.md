# THE LAST VARIABLE — Character Roster

Every power activates by **drawing and solving a problem** from the listed skill deck at the player's tier. Correct = power fires. Wrong = turn ends.

---

## CIRCUIT BREAKERS — pick 1 of 8 (the team)

### 1. The Hacker
- **Power: Override** — Instantly bring one generator online, or open one locked exit, from an adjacent node.
- **Solve:** Algebra · **Cooldown:** 2 rounds
- *"There's always a backdoor."*

### 2. The Medic
- **Power: Reboot** — Revive one frozen teammate within 2 nodes (after its 1-round freeze lockout).
- **Solve:** Percentages · **Cooldown:** 2 rounds
- *"Stay with me — you're not deleted yet."*

### 3. The Scout
- **Power: Ping** — Reveal 3 hidden map tiles and the Glitch's exact location this turn.
- **Solve:** Geometry · **Cooldown:** 1 round
- *"I see everything in here."*

### 4. The Engineer
- **Power: Barricade** — Jam a door or corridor for 2 rounds. The Glitch can't pass it.
- **Solve:** Fractions · **Cooldown:** 2 rounds
- *"Try getting through THAT."*

### 5. The Runner
- **Power: Dash** — Move +3 extra nodes this turn (6 total).
- **Solve:** Mixed (any deck, quick problem) · **Cooldown:** 1 round
- *"Catch me."*

### 6. The Decoder
- **Power: Translate** — Look at one teammate's split-information clue and read it to the table. **Once per game.**
- **Solve:** Any deck · **Use:** 1× per game
- *"Hand me that — I'll read it."* (The quiet lifeline for a stuck teammate — no one has to say they're stuck.)

### 7. The Guardian
- **Power: Firewall** — Block one freeze against yourself or an adjacent teammate.
- **Solve:** Fractions · **Cooldown:** 2 rounds
- *"Not on my watch."*

### 8. The Trickster
- **Power: Decoy** — Drop a fake signal on any node. The Glitch must move toward it next turn, wasting the move.
- **Solve:** Geometry · **Cooldown:** 2 rounds
- *"Look over there. No — over THERE."*

---

## THE GLITCH — pick 1 of 3 (the hunter)

The Glitch's problems are **Tier 3 and timed** (30 seconds on the round timer). Harder math, solo, under pressure — the role that finally challenges the kid who's bored because everything's too easy.

### A. The Corruptor
- **Power: Lockdown** — Seal one exit or freeze one map tile (no one can stand on it) for 2 rounds.
- **Solve:** Algebra (Tier 3, timed) · **Cooldown:** 2 rounds
- *"This door is mine now."*

### B. The Stalker
- **Power: Trace** — Reveal the nearest Breaker's location and move +2 toward them immediately.
- **Solve:** Geometry (Tier 3, timed) · **Cooldown:** 1 round
- *"I can smell the math on you."*

### C. The Overclock
- **Power: Surge** — Take one extra full turn right now.
- **Solve:** Mixed (Tier 3, timed) · **Cooldown:** 2 rounds
- *"Faster. Faster. FASTER."*

## Art notes

All 11 characters are drawn in the locked house style (see `art/style-guide.md`): flat cartoon, bold outlines, neon-on-dark cyber palette. Each card needs **full-body art** + a **cropped token icon** (same drawing, head-and-shoulders) for the board. Per-character generation prompts live in the style guide.
